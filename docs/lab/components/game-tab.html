One Shift

You drive for a ride-hail platform for one three-hour evening shift. The dispatcher sends you trip offers at a fixed, upfront price. Take the good ones, pass on the bad ones, and see how your earnings compare with the other drivers. A shift takes between two and five minutes to play.

How it works
  • Your car is the one with the dark ring. The city is 12 km across, and time speeds up while you're on a trip.
  • When the dispatcher picks you for a trip, the city freezes and an offer appears: the price, the pickup distance, and the trip. Accept with → or Enter, decline with ← or Esc. If time runs out, the offer goes to another driver.
  • You are paid only for the trip itself. The drive to the pickup is unpaid, and your car costs $0.30 a km to run whether or not you have a rider.
  • Your score is your net earnings per hour over the whole shift, compared with four automated drivers who follow simple rules and with everyone else on the road.
