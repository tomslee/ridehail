Just One More Shift…

You drive for a ride-hail platform for one three-hour evening shift. The dispatcher sends you trip offers at a fixed, upfront price. Take the good ones, pass on the bad ones, and see how your earnings compare with the other drivers. A shift takes between two and five minutes to play.

Big city. An 18 km square with Toronto's longer trips and about 6,000 cars in every market (more riders when Busy, fewer when Slow), drawn as a density map with your car on top. Big-city shifts aren't on the leaderboard. Press + again for the usual city.

How it works
  • Your car is the one with the violet ring. The city is 12 km across, and time speeds up while you're on a trip.
  • When the dispatcher picks you for a trip, the city freezes and an offer appears: the price, the pickup distance, and the trip. Accept with → or Enter, decline with ← or Esc. If time runs out, the offer goes to another driver. With the rate helper, the card also shows what the offer pays per km and per hour. Each choice has its own leaderboard.
  • Pause (or the space bar) stops the clock at any time, even with an offer showing, so you can take a closer look. A shift in which you paused an offer can't go on the leaderboard.
  • Each offer is a fixed price for the pickup and the trip together. A long pickup raises the offer a little, but time spent idle is never paid, and your car costs $0.56 a km to run whether or not you have a rider.
  • Your score is your net earnings per hour over the whole shift, compared with four automated drivers who follow simple rules and with everyone else on the road.

Your car costs $0.56 for every km it drives, with or without a rider. That comes to about $12 an hour while you're driving to a pickup or with a rider. Idle cars wait where they are about half the time and cruise the rest (often back towards the City Centre, where most requests start), so an idle hour costs about $6. $0.56 is the median cost per km driven in a 2024 report to the City of Toronto (page 26). It includes both fixed and variable expenses, though the report notes that most of the cost is variable.
